    Study Tip

    Write down every step, even the easy ones. Most algebra mistakes are a dropped negative sign, and they are much easier to find on paper than in your head. When you finish, plug your answer back into the original equation to check it.

    Solve for the unknown

    Algebra is the foundation for almost every math class that comes after it. The four formulas below are the ones students lean on most: solving linear equations, finding the slope of a line, factoring trinomials, and the quadratic formula.
