Geometry

Measure shapes and space

Geometry turns shapes into numbers. These four formulas cover right triangles, the area of a triangle, circles, and the volume of a cylinder, which together show up in a huge number of homework and test problems.

Geometry formulas

    Study tip

    Draw a picture and label everything you know before you pick a formula. Keep units on every number: lengths are plain units, areas are squared (cm²), and volumes are cubed (cm³).

    About Complex Math

    Complex Math is a WDD 231 team project by Agustina Echegaray and David Lunceford. We break the formulas high school and college students use most into small, clear steps, so you can see exactly where every number comes from.
